Short description (max 100 words):

Description:
First Principles turns calculus into a place you can stand on.

2D: 59 stages from Maclaurin series to Riemann sums, AP Calculus BC, aerospace curves and Big O, with stars, daily challenges and hints. The platforms follow f(x); the gaps follow its derivative.

New in 1.5, 3D graphing: plot surfaces z = f(x, y), curves (x(t), y(t), z(t)), parametric surfaces and implicit shapes like x² + y² + z² = 9. Orbit, zoom, tap any point for its coordinates and partial derivatives, and drag sliders a, b, c.

13 3D stages: shape planes, bowls, saddles, waves, helix rails and a spiral staircase, then walk your own graph from the start pad to the goal.

Keyboard and mouse, touch, and Xbox controllers. 14 interface languages.
